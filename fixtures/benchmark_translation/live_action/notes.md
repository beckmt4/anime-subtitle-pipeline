# Fixture: live_action

## Description

Short synthetic live-action Japanese dialogue: polite workplace conversation
following a meeting. Uses keigo (honorific speech) forms which are often
mistranslated as overly formal or stiff English.

## Source

Synthetic — not derived from any copyrighted work.

## Expected translation challenges

- Polite suffix `-ます / -でした` endings often rendered awkwardly
- `おかげです` (thanks-to) idiomatic credit expression
- `なかなか大変` (quite difficult) — degree adverb nuance

## Usage

Compare engines on their handling of polite Japanese registers. A good
translation should use natural-sounding business English, not stilted
literal renderings.
